Pacing and rubric

8–10 weeks

  1. What is 3D printing, and safety
  2. How a printer works, and materials
  3. CAD, part 1
  4. CAD, part 2
  5. Libraries and slicing
  6. First prints and troubleshooting
  7. VEX practice lab (robotics students) or design iteration
  8. Idea to business, pitch sheet
  9. Take-home print queue
  10. Assemble, test, show, certificate

Score 1–4

Criterion4 looks like
ProblemA real person and a clear need
Design fileOriginal or credited, in millimeters
SliceLayer height, infill, and supports chosen on purpose
AssemblyPins fit with the planned 0.2–0.3 mm clearance
TestOne change after a real test
SafetyHot parts, ventilation, and pom-poms rules followed
HonestyNo fake patent language; licenses respected
