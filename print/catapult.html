Grades 7–8 take-home

Crank-Gear Mini Catapult

Pom-poms or a soft foam ball only. Aim at a box. Never at a person. A teacher watches.

Ratio = 36 ÷ 12 = 3. Module 1.5 mm. Center distance 36 mm. Axle holes 5.3 mm for a 5.0 mm axle (0.3 mm clearance).

  1. Calculate the ratio before you wind anything.
  2. Mesh the gears. They turn; they do not lock or skip.
  3. Crank on the axle flat. Hinge pin in the arm. Pick a stop hole.
  4. Personalized plate in the base recess.
  5. One pom-pom test. Move the stop. Test again. Write the change.

Rubric marks

Ratio shown · fit tested · one iteration · launch safety · honest pitch of what the machine is not.
