Printer checklist

Printer number ____ Job ____________________ Student initials ________

Before

  • Bed is clear and cool enough to wipe.
  • Filament end is in hand, not loose on the spool.
  • File name matches the queue.
  • Teacher watched the first layer start.

After

  • Printer is finished or stopped. Nozzle not touched.
  • Part removed by a teacher or with coaching.
  • Bed wiped if the surface allows it.
  • Queue marked done or failed, with a reason if it failed.
