Lesson plans

Each block is one module. Grades 5–6 stay concrete. Grades 7–8 add the number or the rule.

1. What is 3D printing?

Objectives: Tell additive from subtractive. Name one real use without science fiction.

Materials: Course page, paper circles.

Warm-up: Stack three paper circles into a dome.

Activity: Timeline and the two pictures. 7–8 compare waste.

Exit ticket: Additive means ______.

2. How a printer works

Objectives: Point to spool, extruder, hotend, nozzle, gantry, and bed.

Materials: Cold printer or the diagram. No hands on a hot nozzle.

Warm-up: Which part melts plastic?

Activity: Click the diagram. 7–8 compute layers for a 12 mm part.

Exit ticket: The extruder pushes. The hotend ______.

3. Safety

Objectives: Name heat, air, hair, and who uses the scraper.

Materials: Safety contract.

Warm-up: Spot the hazard on the course picture.

Activity: Sign the contract. Walk the room with you leading.

Exit ticket: One rule I will follow tomorrow.

4. Materials

Objectives: Choose PLA for school and say why ABS is not the default.

Materials: Labeled PLA spool. No loose ABS in the room.

Warm-up: Which filament bends?

Activity: Job picker on the site. 7–8 read a spool label.

Exit ticket: PLA is our default because ______.

5. CAD

Objectives: Make a solid with a hole and export STL.

Materials: Tinkercad class. 7–8 may open Fusion or Onshape.

Warm-up: Extrude slider on the course page.

Activity: Name tag or parametric pin.

Exit ticket: A hole is a shape you ______.

6. Model libraries

Objectives: Read a license and write a credit line.

Materials: Printables or Thingiverse on a projector.

Warm-up: What does NC mean?

Activity: License decoder. Write one credit strip.

Exit ticket: I may sell a print only if ______.

7. Slicing

Objectives: Explain layer height, infill, and supports.

Materials: PrusaSlicer, Cura, Bambu Studio, or OrcaSlicer.

Warm-up: Course estimator on a keychain.

Activity: Slice the same STL twice. Record the slicer’s numbers.

Exit ticket: The slicer, not the classroom estimate, is the number I trust because ______.

8. Troubleshooting

Objectives: Name stringing, warping, spaghetti, and a bad first layer.

Materials: Photos only, or a cooled failed print you hold.

Warm-up: Which picture is hairs between towers?

Activity: One variable. Students do not turn knobs alone.

Exit ticket: I would ask a teacher to change ______ first.

9. VEX Robotics

Objectives: Use 12.7 mm pitch and label the part practice.

Materials: Real IQ pin or #8-32 screw. Current game manual link.

Warm-up: Tap three holes on the grid.

Activity: Practice cable guide or sensor mount. Not for the match robot unless you checked the manual.

Exit ticket: Official IQ snap hole is ______ mm.

10. Business

Objectives: Compare make-and-sell with licensing. Price after costs.

Materials: Pitch sheet. Calculator on the site.

Warm-up: Riley’s fictional hook. What path is which?

Activity: Fill the pitch. 7–8 add the one-page plan.

Exit ticket: Patent pending means ______.

11. Capstone and take-home

Objectives: Print, assemble, test, and change one thing.

Materials: Starter STLs, PLA, pom-poms for 7–8, planning sheet.

Warm-up: Which clearance is a swivel, 0.2 or 0.3 mm?

Activity: Queue the jobs. Test. Write the change.

Exit ticket: One thing I improved and how I know.
