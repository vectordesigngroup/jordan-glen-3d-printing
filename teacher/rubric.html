Grading rubric

Score each column 1 to 4. 4 is evidence a visitor can see. 1 is missing.

ScoreDesignProcessPrintingPresentation
4The shape matches a real need. Sizes are in millimeters.Notes show a test and one change.Settings were chosen. The part matches the plan closely enough to use.The pitch names the user, the path (make and sell or license), and what is not claimed.
3The shape works, with one unclear size.A test is written. The change is thin.It printed. A small flaw is explained.The story is clear and honest.
2A model exists. The need is vague.Steps are listed without a result.It printed once with no note about settings.Shows the object and stops.
1No file, or a file the student cannot explain.No notes.Did not print and there is no plan to.Missing, or claims a patent that does not exist.

Safety is a gate, not extra credit. A student who breaks the hot-nozzle or pom-pom rule revisits the safety lesson before the next lab.
