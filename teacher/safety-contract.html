Maker space safety contract

Jordan Glen School. I agree to:

  • Not touch the nozzle, hotend, or a hot bed.
  • Keep hair, hoods, and sleeves away from belts.
  • Stand back while a printer runs, and tell a teacher if I smell hot plastic.
  • Keep food and drinks off the printer table.
  • Let a teacher use the scraper and cutters.
  • Print PLA unless a teacher chooses another material.
  • Launch only soft pom-poms, aimed at a box, never at a person.
