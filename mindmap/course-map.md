# Jordan Glen 3D Printing Course

## [1. What is 3D printing?](#what-is-3d-printing)

- Add layers, do not carve
- Less scrap than cutting
- Hull, Crump, RepRap, ISS
- Models, tools, houses, food
- [Lesson video](assets/videos/what-is-3d-printing.mp4)

## [2. How a printer works](#how-a-printer-works)

- Filament on a spool
- Extruder pushes
- Hotend melts
- Nozzle draws
- Gantry moves
- Bed holds the part
- [Lesson video](assets/videos/how-a-printer-works.mp4)

## [3. Safety](#safety)

- Hot nozzle and bed
- Fresh air, do not hover
- Hair and sleeves back
- Teacher uses the scraper
- PLA in this room
- [School printer safety](https://doh.wa.gov/community-and-environment/schools/3d-printers)
- [NIOSH makerspace guide](https://www.cdc.gov/niosh/docs/2024-103/pdfs/2024-103.pdf)
- [Lesson video](assets/videos/safety.mp4)

## [4. Materials](#materials)

- PLA is the school default
- PETG is tougher
- TPU can bend
- ABS needs special air
- Not for food or a hot car
- [Lesson video](assets/videos/materials.mp4)

## [11. Capstone and take-home lab](#capstone)

### The big project

- Design, print, test, improve
- 8 to 10 weeks
- Checklist saved on this device
- [Capstone video](assets/videos/capstone.mp4)
- [Take-home video](assets/videos/take-home-project.mp4)

### [Desk Buddy Bot, grades 5–6](#take-home-project)

- Pen cup and phone stand
- Swivel head, arms, feet
- Name plate in Tinkercad
- About 8–12 cm tall

### [Crank-Gear Catapult, grades 7–8](#take-home-project)

- 12 teeth driving 36
- Ratio 3 to 1
- 0.2–0.3 mm pin clearance
- Pom-poms only, aimed at a box
