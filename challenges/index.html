Weekly challenges

Constraints make the design smarter

Nine prompts. Score each from 1 to 4 on the card. Print a card from the browser. Finished pieces can go in the gallery under initials only.

1. Light phone stand

Constraint: holds a phone and the slicer estimate is under 20 g. Flat on the bed, no supports if you can.

Use Tinkercad. Three shapes maximum.

Record grams, time, and one change after a tip test on a table.

Score: stands (1), under 20 g (1), does not tip with a gentle push (1), you explain one improvement (1).

2. Penny bridge

Constraint: span at least 80 mm between two books. Pennies stay in a tray. No launching them.

Count how many pennies it holds before it bends.

Report pennies per gram. Stronger is not the only win. Lighter can win too.

Score: spans the gap (1), holds at least 10 pennies (1), you measured grams (1), you say why the shape works (1).

3. Tallest tower per gram

Constraint: stands on its own. Height in millimeters divided by grams is the score number. It must not be a solid brick.

Print it upright or on its side on purpose, and say which is stronger.

Score: stands (1), you measured height and grams (1), infill is not 100% (1), you name the weak layer (1).

4. Marble maze tile

Constraint: one tile, about 80 mm square, walls a marble can follow. Test over a box, not across the room.

Score: marble stays in the path (1), start and finish are marked (1), no supports left on (1), a classmate can solve it (1).

5. VEX practice part

Constraint: holes on a 12.7 mm pitch. Label the file PRACTICE. It does not go on a competition robot unless the current manual allows it.

Two IQ snap holes, tested with a real pin.

A sensor mount or cable guide. Write the hole size you tested.

Score: pitch is right (1), pin or screw fits (1), labeled practice (1), you cite the manual check (1).

6. Gear fidget

Constraint: two gears that turn on pins. Clearance 0.2 to 0.3 mm. No sharp teeth facing a hand.

Write the tooth ratio, driven divided by driver.

Score: they mesh (1), they turn (1), clearance is written down (1), one reprint note (1).

7. Three-cord clip

Constraint: holds three cords or strings without pinching a finger. PLA is fine for a first try.

Score: holds three (1), lets go without breaking (1), under 10 g if you can (1), edges are smooth (1).

8. Standing name tag

Constraint: your initials, and it stands on a desk without tape. Grades 5–6 stay in Tinkercad.

Score: letters readable (1), stands (1), a hole or slot was planned (1), you credit any remix (1).

9. Soft drop keychain

Constraint: a keychain with a hole. Drop it from desk height onto a towel, not onto tile, and not at a person.

Score: hole fits a ring (1), survives one drop (1), you name the layer that might split (1), grams are recorded (1).
