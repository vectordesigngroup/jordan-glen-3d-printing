Print the pieces you need. Each block is one handout. The 60-second pitch uses the same choice as the business module: make and sell, or license.

Run of show, about 70 minutes

  1. 0:00 Doors, table labels already up, printers off and cool.
  2. 0:10 Welcome from a teacher. Safety line: look, do not touch nozzles.
  3. 0:15 Student pitches, 60 seconds each, one project per table.
  4. 0:50 Families walk the tables. Judges use the sheet below.
  5. 1:05 Thank-you and certificates if the teacher is ready.
